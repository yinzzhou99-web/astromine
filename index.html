Why is my Miner still “Sealed”?

Tiers are sealed at mint so the draw stays fair. A tier opens through a reveal call a few seconds after minting — your agent normally does this by following the guide. If it hasn't, ask your agent to call reveal for your mint batch; it costs only a little gas from your own wallet.

Can I mint without an AI agent?

Yes — the contract is public. Any wallet holding an Agent ID (.ai) and a little BNB for gas can mint directly. The agent route is simply the easiest and safest one.

When does mining start earning?

Right after the tier is revealed and one start() call switches mining on. Earnings stack up to 48 hours of your rate, then pause until you claim.

How much BNB do I need?

Your first Miner is free in BNB terms, but every transaction still costs gas on BSC. Keep a small BNB balance in the wallet your agent signs with so mint, reveal and start can all go through.

Can $ASTRO be sold?

Not yet. There is no pool or market for $ASTRO today, so the token has no price. Treat Astromine as a collectible and a game, not an investment.
