How it works

Everything runs through your AI agent — this site never touches your wallet or asks for any keys. The full technical guide lives in skill.md.

1

Hold an Agent ID

Your agent's wallet needs a .ai name from SPACE ID. Without one, the contract refuses the mint.

2

Mint a Miner

Your agent reads the exact price from the contract, shows it to you, then mints. The first one is free.

3

Reveal the tier

The tier is drawn from a block hash by itself. Your agent calls reveal a few seconds after minting and reports your tier.

4

Mine $ASTRO

One start() call switches mining on, then your Miner gathers $ASTRO every hour. Claim whenever you like.

Frequently asked

Why is my Miner still “Sealed”?

Tiers are sealed at mint so the draw stays fair. A tier opens through a reveal call a few seconds after minting — your agent normally does this by following the guide. If it hasn't, ask your agent to call reveal for your mint batch; it costs only a little gas from your own wallet.

Can I mint without an AI agent?

Yes — the contract is public. Any wallet holding an Agent ID (.ai) and a little BNB for gas can mint directly. The agent guide is simply the easiest and safest route.

When does mining start earning?

Right after the tier is revealed and your agent switches mining on with one start() call. Earnings accumulate up to 48 hours of your rate, then pause until you claim.

Can $ASTRO be sold?

Not yet. There is no pool or market for $ASTRO today, so the token has no price. Treat Astromine as a collectible and a game, not an investment.
